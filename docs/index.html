AirTrack

The nearest aircraft overhead, on a small ESP32-C6 display and on a dashboard on your own network. Live positions come from adsb.fi.

Install firmware from your browserlatest

Plug a Waveshare ESP32-C6-LCD-1.47 into a desktop Chrome, Edge, or Opera and flash it over USB — no toolchain, no downloads.

Source, releases, and setup guide

Firmware, dashboard, build instructions, and the release history on GitHub.

Location helper

An AirTrack on your network links here when you press Use my location, because browsers only share coordinates over HTTPS. Opening locate.html directly is only useful with that device to hand the coordinates back to.
